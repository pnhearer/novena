# Commercial licences

novena is free under two public licences. [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) explains them in a table.

You need a commercial licence when you want to use novena commercially and you do not want to follow the GPL. In practice that means making money with it while keeping your own source closed. For example:

- shipping novena, or anything built from it, in or with a closed product that is sold,
- preinstalling it on hardware that is sold, without giving buyers the complete source and the ability to replace the software,
- offering it as part of a paid, closed service.

You do not need a commercial licence if your use is noncommercial, or if you follow the GPL in full for everything you distribute with novena.

To ask about a commercial licence, email pnhearer@digital-haven.net with who you are and what you want to do. Terms are agreed case by case.

If you are not sure which side you are on, ask. Asking costs nothing.
